# Fight Club — What Went Viral vs What Didn't

> Data: YouTube Studio Advanced Analytics export, lifetime to Oct 4, 2026 (85 shorts, 11.5M views, +4,244 subs). The deep editing comparison covers the 15 most-viewed and 15 least-viewed shorts. The two newest shorts (Oct 3–4) were excluded from the "bottom" group because they're too young to judge.
> Note: Studio counts every play/loop as a "View"; "Engaged views" is the old-style view count. Engaged/Views ≈ 0.43–0.65 across the channel.

## Headline numbers
- **Hit-driven channel:** the top 5 shorts = **53%** of all views, and the top 11 = 75%. Median short = **36K** views.
- The **top 5 brought 2,588 of the 4,244 subs (61%)**. Viral shorts are where the subscribers come from.
- **Garcia vs Benn storyline** (7 titles with "Benn") = **3.49M views (30%)** with 3 of the top 7 shorts. Counting every Ryan Garcia short, the arc is ~48% of the channel.

## Finding 1: The editing is NOT what separates hits from flops
Top-15 vs bottom-15 medians:

| Metric | Top 15 | Bottom 15 |
|---|---|---|
| Engaged/Views (rough "stayed" proxy) | 0.53 | 0.51 |
| Avg % viewed | 100.8% | 99.3% |
| Like rate (likes / engaged view) | 3.2% | 3.0% |
| Cuts | 12 | 8 |
| Avg shot length | 2.55 s | 2.59 s |
| First cut | 1.77 s | 2.27 s |
| Duration | 32.5 s | 26.6 s |

Across all 85 shorts, the correlation between log(views) and avg % viewed is **0.07**, and with engaged ratio it's **−0.01**. Retention is already strong everywhere (most shorts loop at about 100%), so the house style is solid. **Views are decided by topic, timing and story more than by editing tweaks.**

Small editing edges the hits *do* have:
- Slightly more cuts (12 vs 8) and an earlier first cut (1.8 s vs 2.3 s)
- Longer runtime (30–40 s) with a real arc. The very short ones (13–20 s) flopped.
- Fewer blur transitions (≈1 per 10 s vs ≈2.7) and more white-flash accents on big beats
- **Captions on every hit.** The three caption-less early shorts checked all flopped.

## Finding 2: What the viral shorts share
1. **A live rivalry at peak news moment.** #1 "Conor Benn SHUTS UP Ryan Garcia" (1.62M) dropped right around the weigh-in (Benn made 146 lbs). #2 "Paddy Got FINED For Celebrating" (1.42M) dropped right after the UFC event. #3 "Ryan Garcia Got ASSAULTED By Conor Benn" (1.10M) came off the presser scuffle.
2. **Claim → counter → receipts.** Someone says X, the other side fires back, then the proof lands (146 lbs on the scale, the KO, the belt). The payoff sits at the end, so viewers stay and the video loops.
3. **Controversy, identity or money hooks:** "FAKE MEXICAN", "SCAMMED … $40K", "BANNED", "FINED", "ASSAULTED", "HALF BLACK".
4. **"Who's better" debates** with a credible narrator: "Prime Khabib vs Prime Islam" (DC, 1.07M), "Prime GSP vs Islam" (Bisping, 265K).
5. **Emotion:** "Islam's EMOTIONAL tribute to Khabib's father" (410K) has few cuts (avg shot 6.5 s), but the story carries it.

## Finding 3: Why the flops flopped
| Pattern | Examples (views) |
|---|---|
| **Early channel, no captions, watermark only, long static holds** | Conor Is About To DESTROY Max (1.7K), Conor is in his PRIME now (5.2K), Conor SLAPPED Max (4.3K, 13 s, blurry upscale) |
| **Off-niche** | FIFA leaked Spain's winning SCRIPT (1.7K; soccer), the man behind Cody Rhodes SUCCESS (9.9K; WWE) |
| **Long wide fight footage in a letterbox strip** | the FUNNIEST boxing fight of all time (3.7K, 48 s, 55% viewed; lowest retention on the channel) |
| **Same storyline repeated too often** | 7 Islam-vs-Ian Garry shorts in 2 weeks (Aug 4–17), sliding from 45K down to 9K |
| **Speculation with no event/payoff** | "Conor is gonna KNOCKOUT Max in first round?!" (8.6K), "Anthony joshua career is OVER" (12K; 6+ random speakers, no single arc) |
| **Old story, no news peg** | "Khabib HATED Mike Tyson for this" (1.4K): same editing quality as the hits, but no reason to care today |
| **McGregor content in July** | 17 McGregor shorts, median 25.5K, far below the Garcia/Benn median of 56K |

Channel age matters too. July uploads have the lowest median (17K first half), rising to 72K in early September as the channel grew. Bottom-15 is skewed toward July, so some of the flop gap comes from that.

## Recommendations
1. **Ride the hottest rivalry of the week** and post within 24 h of the moment: presser, weigh-in, fight night, fine or suspension. A big event is worth 3–6 shorts across its build-up, weigh-in, result and fallout (like Garcia–Benn), but **cap a single angle at ~3 shorts** to avoid the Ian Garry slide.
2. **Always have a payoff.** If there's no receipt yet (speculation), it's a weaker idea.
3. **Hook in frame 1** with the most inflammatory line, and make the first cut by 1.5 s.
4. Keep the house style: serif captions with a colored keyword, a tight face reframe with push-in, hard cuts, 30–40 s.
5. **Avoid:** off-niche sports, long wide fight footage, 6+ unrelated talking heads, shorts under 20 s, and stories with no news peg.
6. Titles: `Name + CAPS action + Name ?! + 😳` works. "vs" titles have a 56K median vs 34K for the rest.
